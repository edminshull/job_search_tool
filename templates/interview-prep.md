## How to use this

Work it in two passes. First pass: read the questions and say your answer out
loud, without notes. Anything that comes out vague or rambling is where you need
a sharper story. Second pass: tighten the stories below and rehearse the numbers
until they're automatic.

Everything here is drawn from facts already in `cv/master.yaml`. If a question
has no honest answer in your history, it is listed under **Genuine gaps** — do
not invent one in the room.
